About Lucky Leo's Rescue

This rescue was inspired by my own rescue dog, Leo. He was returned multiple times before he found his furever home with me. Now he is living his best life, making him Lucky Leo.

Lucky Leo's Rescue is a nonprofit dog rescue. We take in dogs from local shelters, care for them in foster homes, and help them find permanent homes. Many of our dogs are purebred, so you can find the breed you want without going to a breeder.
